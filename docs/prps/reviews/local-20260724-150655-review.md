## Summary

The implementation follows the planned filter → select → sequence → output boundary and passes
focused, coverage, type, build, formatting, markdown, completion, and graph checks. Review found
two blocking scheduler issues: the initial backtracking window can relax a feasible schedule, and
the candidate hot path repeatedly rescans and sorts enough state to make thousand-track libraries
too slow.

## Findings

### CRITICAL

None.

### HIGH

- **[F001]** `packages/rfmedia-cli/src/lib/separation.ts:238` — The fixed four-track
  backtracking window can relax a gap even when the complete catalog has a valid ordering. Artists
  `C,B,C,D,D,D,B` with `artist: 2` relaxes the final placement although
  `D,C,B,D,C,B,D` satisfies the policy.
  - **Status**: Fixed
  - **Category**: Correctness
  - **Suggested fix**: Progressively widen the movable suffix within a fixed node budget before
    relaxing, and add the seven-track catalog as a regression test.

- **[F002]** `packages/rfmedia-cli/src/lib/separation.ts:298` — Every output position filters and
  sorts all remaining candidates while each comparison rescans selected tracks and repeatedly
  normalizes metadata. A 1,000-track local benchmark took approximately nine seconds.
  - **Status**: Fixed
  - **Category**: Performance
  - **Suggested fix**: Precompute normalized keys and BPM bands, maintain last-seen keys, decade
    counts, and the previous BPM band incrementally, and choose the best eligible candidate in one
    pass rather than sorting at every position.

### MEDIUM

None.

### LOW
